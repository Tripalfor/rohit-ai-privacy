Rohit AI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Rohit AI is a personal AI assistant project. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when using its features.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, Rohit AI may process Instagram account information, messages, comments and related interaction data.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide AI-generated responses, manage Instagram interactions and display message history.

4. Data Storage

Rohit AI is designed to run on a local computer. Information stored by the application may remain in its local database. Meta may separately process information according to its own privacy policies.

5. Sharing of Information

Rohit AI does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by Meta services when Instagram API features are used.

6. Data Security

Users should protect their computer, application credentials and access tokens. Access tokens and passwords should never be shared publicly.

7. Data Deletion

Users can request information about stored data and its deletion by contacting the project owner. Local application data may also require deletion from the computer where Rohit AI is installed.

8. Third-Party Services

Instagram and Meta services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Rohit AI project owner using the contact email associated with the project.
